R$1,440 ≠ R$400 + R$960

The simulated legacy system divided the R$240 increase across the original 12 installments but changed only the eight future installments. LDM detects an R$80 shortfall. The example rule requires allocating the increase across the eight remaining installments.

The mathematical analysis is deterministic. The optional AI module generates separate hypotheses subject to human review. The default demo does not call a model.

How to interpret this result

The demo command uses known experimental answers to simulate decisions. It does not establish real human review, regulatory compliance or measured improvements at a real insurer. Use generate, run and review to operate the workflow manually.
